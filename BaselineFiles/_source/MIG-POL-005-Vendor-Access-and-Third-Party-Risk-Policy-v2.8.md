# MIG-POL-005 — Vendor Access and Third-Party Risk Policy

**Document ID:** MIG-POL-005  
**Version:** v2.8  
**Effective:** October 2025  
**Owner:** GRC Office (with Vendor Management)  

---

## §1 Purpose

Governs third-party vendor access to MIG systems, identity provisioning, geographic restrictions, and remote-support session controls.

## §5 Geo Restrictions

ZTNA restrictions limited to India and US only are non-compliant. All eight approved vendor countries from MIG-POL-003 §4 must be permitted.

## §6 Remote Support

All vendor remote-support sessions must be logged to SIEM. TeamViewer, AnyDesk, BeyondTrust are the approved tools (see MIG-POL-001 §2.3).

## §SANC01 Sanctions Compliance

OFAC-sanctioned countries remain blocked regardless of vendor policy. Sanctions controls take precedence over access permissions.
